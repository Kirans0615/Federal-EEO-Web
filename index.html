Federal EEO, LLC is a Washington, DC-based federal employment law practice led by Ericka Guthrie Dorsey, Esq. With over 20 years of specialized experience and a JD from The George Washington University Law School, Ericka guides federal employees through every stage of the EEO process — from the critical 45-day counselor contact window through EEOC administrative hearings and beyond. A Commissioner on the ABA Commission on Disability Rights, she brings both rigorous legal strategy and genuine advocacy to every matter.
